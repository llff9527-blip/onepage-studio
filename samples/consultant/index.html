Selected work

Three invented engagements, written the way a real case study would read. The companies below do not exist.

01 — Goods

Halden Goods

A new homewares label needed a position that was not “minimal” or “artisan.” We defined a quieter claim — tools for a well-kept house — and a messaging system for the first collection and site.

Positioning · site narrative

02 — Studio

North Quay Studio

An architecture practice was winning work through referrals and losing it on the website. We rewrote the offer, trimmed six service lines to three, and gave principals language they would actually use in pitches.

Offer design · pitch language

A fictional independent library wanted neighbors, not tourists. The work was a plain-spoken story, a short membership page, and a set of lines for posters and a monthly letter.

Services

    A clear claim, a defined audience, and the lines you will not cross.

  • Messaging systems

    Homepage narrative, offer language, and a short voice guide your team can reuse.

    The structure and copy for a one-page or short-form site — ready to design and build.

Process

  1. 01

    Discover

    A structured conversation about what you sell, who it is for, and where the current story slips.

  2. 02

    Distill

    A written position, a map of the page, and draft lines — reviewed together, then tightened.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Final copy, a simple usage note, and a handover you can hand to a designer or keep as-is.

About this sample

A practice that exists only on this page.

Ada Moreau is a fictional independent brand strategist, created by OnePage Studio to show what a consultant’s one-page site can feel like: calm typography, a clear offer, and work samples that read as specific rather than generic.

In this story she works remotely with small teams in North America and Europe, takes a limited number of projects at a time, and prefers companies that already have something good — they just have not said it plainly yet.

All clients, testimonials-as-case-studies, and contact details on this page are invented. This is a demonstration site, not a real practice.
